EVENTUAL CONSISTENCY WITH ASYNCHRONOUS REPLICATION

1. A normal write on the primary became visible on the replica after about 123 ms (local Docker polling; not a production latency figure).

2. Replica WAL replay was paused with pg_wal_replay_pause(), then a row was committed on the primary.
   rows named 'while-replay-paused' on the primary: 1
   rows named 'while-replay-paused' on the replica: 0   <- stale read
   Primary's view of the lag:
    application_name |   state   | sync_state | replay_backlog_bytes 
   ------------------+-----------+------------+----------------------
    replica1         | streaming | async      |                  280
   (1 row)

3. Replay was resumed. The replica caught up and now returns the row: 1

PASS: reads from an asynchronous replica can be stale, and they converge once WAL is replayed.
